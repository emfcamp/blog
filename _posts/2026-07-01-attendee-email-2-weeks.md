## Getting into the UK

If you are travelling from the EU you probably don't need to get a visa, but you probably **DO** need to get an ETA: [https://www.gov.uk/eta](https://www.gov.uk/eta)

This is a new requirement since the last EMF - make sure you apply at least 3 working days before you set off. If you're unsure whether you require a visa [please check](https://www.gov.uk/check-uk-visa).

## Electromagnetic Telecom

Mobile phone coverage at the festival is poor, so our own Telecommunications provider provides free wireless and wired services to the entire site - everything from phone calls, to fax, to dial-up internet access for your Apple II. 

To use the phone network you'll need to bring a compatible device such as a [cordless DECT landline phone](https://phones.emfcamp.org/help/dect), [mobile phone](https://phones.emfcamp.org/help/gsm) with a spare SIM slot, [SIP device](https://phones.emfcamp.org/help/sip), or [old-school wired phone](https://phones.emfcamp.org/help/pots), fax machine, or modem.

You can register your number now on [phones.emfcamp.org](https://phones.emfcamp.org). If you have questions about connecting please [contact us](mailto:phones@emfcamp.org).

## Join a village

Villages are groups of people who want to camp together, for any reason: friend groups, real-world clubs and organisations, or just people with a common interest. You can find [registered villages](https://www.emfcamp.org/villages), or [register your own](https://www.emfcamp.org/about/villages) on the website.

If you'd like to join an existing village, you can request to join some villages by clicking "Request to Join". This will send the village your email address, so they can pass on details if they're happy for you to join them. Villages are under no obligation to take on members, and if there is no "Request to Join" button it means the village is private.
