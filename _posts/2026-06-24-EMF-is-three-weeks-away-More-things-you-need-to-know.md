Hello,

We're only three weeks away from being in the field and there's more things you need to know!

## EMF runs all Sunday (into the night)

Do not forget that we run for all of Sunday. Talks finish by 7pm, but we have a full schedule of entertainment until midnight!

**You are welcome to remain on site until midday on Monday 20th**.

## Music & Performances are go

We've announced the [live music](https://www.emfcamp.org/schedule/2026#music) and evening comedy [performances](https://www.emfcamp.org/schedule/2026#performance)! The full Null Sector DJ line-up will be announced in the near future.

## More Village tents!

We found a few more marquees down the back of the sofa, so if you'd like a shared space for your village **[order one now](https://emfcamp.org/tickets/hire)**.

It is a very limited number of tents - if the shop says "sold out", regrettably we have already sold out.

## Bring your home phone

EMF has terrible phone signal and we make up for it by running our own network, using commonly available cordless landline DECT phones. You probably already have one.

You can find more information about what phones you can bring [on the comprehensive phones website](https://emfcamp.org/about/phones), and number registration will open soon.


## Wiki Wiki

We've always had a place for you to publish information, and this year it looks a little different due to the onslaught of web scrapers.

You can find it at [https://emfcamp.org/wiki](https://emfcamp.org/wiki), and if you have feedback [we'd love to hear it](mailto:web@emfcamp.org).

## We want your ads

The [EMF Film Festival](https://films.emfcamp.org/) is in full swing and we need your adverts to show between films - show off your mirrored asteroid, advertise your workshop, show us your nerdy holiday pics!

Send 30s long, 1080p, family friendly clips to [films@emfcamp.org](mailto:films@emfcamp.org) for your chance on the big screen.
