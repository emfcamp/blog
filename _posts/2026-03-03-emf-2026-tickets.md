We're happy to announce that the main ticket sales dates for EMF 2026 have been finalised! They will be:

* **Monday 9th March**, 20:00 UTC
* **Sunday 22nd March**, 15:00 UTC
* **Thursday 2nd April**, 18:00 BST

Prices start at £190 for an adult ticket. Tickets will be sold at [www.emfcamp.org/tickets](https://www.emfcamp.org/tickets), and you can see the full list of prices there.

While we have increased the capacity of EMF slightly this year, we expect tickets to sell out quickly, as they have in previous years.

## Volunteer tickets

If you volunteered or spoke at the last EMF in 2024, you are guaranteed a chance to buy a ticket. You will receive a voucher by email before the 22nd of March. Please drop us an email at [tickets@emfcamp.org](mailto:tickets@emfcamp.org) after that date if you haven't received your voucher.

## Call for Participation

If you want to submit a talk or workshop proposal to EMF 2026, our Call for Participation will open soon, and we'll send another email when this happens. If your proposal is accepted, you'll be guaranteed a chance to buy a ticket.

See you in the field!
